# Growth and Marketing

## Studio model
Use one public studio identity: **Knowlly Games**.

Primary web presence: `knowlly.games`.

Do not create separate social accounts or domains for every game by default. Individual brands earn dedicated infrastructure only after breakout traction.

## Initial social channels
- TikTok
- Instagram Reels
- YouTube Shorts

## Creative loop
Game emits interesting moments → replay/capture → edit/template → organic publishing → observe watch/share/click/install performance → promote winning creative into paid acquisition.

## Marketing events
Games may mark moments such as:
- huge combo
- perfect solve
- rare merge
- last-second win
- close save
- massive swarm

These events can feed deterministic replay and future automated capture.

## Remotion direction
Future creative factory:
- replay interesting session
- capture gameplay
- produce 9:16 / 1:1 / 16:9
- generate multiple hooks/captions/CTAs
- schedule organic variants
- convert winners into paid creative

## Paid acquisition
Do not optimize for installs alone.

Evaluate creative/campaign cohorts by:
- CPI
- tutorial completion
- D1 / D7 retention
- sessions
- ad revenue
- IAP revenue
- LTV vs acquisition cost

Keep human approval for meaningful budget escalation.

## Cross-promotion
After multiple games exist, allow tasteful in-portfolio discovery. Do not spam players with unrelated app promotions.
