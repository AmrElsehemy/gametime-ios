# Product Principles

## Core product promise

Knowlly Games ships small iOS games that are immediately understandable, deeply polished, and easy to return to.

## Principles

1. **First interaction in seconds.** No instruction wall before play.
2. **Teach through play.** The first levels are the tutorial.
3. **Short sessions, instant replay.** Restarting or advancing should feel frictionless.
4. **Juice matters.** Input must produce satisfying visual, audio, motion, and haptic feedback.
5. **Progressive novelty.** Introduce new mechanics, blockers, goals, or presentation beats before difficulty becomes stale.
6. **Difficulty is paced, not merely increased.** Alternate learning, practice, challenge, and relief.
7. **Monetize value, not pain.** Rewarded ads and optional commerce enhance the experience; the free game remains complete.
8. **No monetization in the initial teaching flow.** Let the player understand and enjoy the game first.
9. **Offline first.** Core play does not depend on a server, ads, Game Center, or LiveOps.
10. **Public release is the definition of done.** A framework that compiles is not a shipped product.
11. **Extract reuse only after evidence.** Build specific games first; generalize after duplication is proven.
12. **Measure behavior.** Retention, completion, difficulty, monetization, crash, and acquisition decisions are data-informed.
13. **Respect attention.** Build highly engaging games without manipulative dark patterns.
14. **Operate like a studio.** Support, growth, releases, diagnostics, and marketing are product capabilities, not afterthoughts.
