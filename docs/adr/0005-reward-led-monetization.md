# ADR 0005 — Reward-Led Monetization

## Status
Accepted

## Decision
Rewarded ads are the default monetization mechanism where ads make sense. Interstitials, if used, are conservative and centrally frequency-capped. Never degrade the game to sell relief.

## Consequences
- Free play remains complete.
- Initial tutorials are monetization-free.
- Commerce, boosters, cosmetics, passes and stores are opt-in capabilities by title maturity.
